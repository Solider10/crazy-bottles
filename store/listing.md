## Full description
Welcome to Crazy Bottles, a cozy water-sort puzzle set in a glowing Japanese night.

Pour the colorful potions until every bottle holds a single color. Sounds easy? The bottles have their own personalities, and some are frozen or locked!

- 36 handcrafted levels in 3 worlds, plus endless levels
- Frozen bottles that thaw after a few moves
- Locked bottles that open with a key you earn by sorting a bottle
- A Magic helper and hints when you get stuck, plus unlimited undo
- Cute faces that react to every move
- A story about the Great Sakura tree and a mischievous wind spirit
- Relaxing visuals, no timers, no pressure
- Works offline. No ads in this version.
